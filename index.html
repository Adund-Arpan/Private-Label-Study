Methodology

Where these questions come from

Every attitudinal item in the "Quick reactions" section is adapted from peer-reviewed marketing research rather than written from scratch. Screening and demographic questions are standard instrument scaffolding, not measured constructs, so they aren't individually cited.

  1. Manipulation checks — whether the private-label and national-brand products registered as prominently placed, and whether the price difference was noticed — adapted from Long, F., & Amaldoss, W. (2024). Self-preferencing in e-commerce marketplaces: The role of sponsored advertising and private labels. Marketing Science, 43(5), 925–952; and Choi, S. C., & Turut, O. (2023). National brand's competition with premium private labels: The role of context-dependent preferences. International Journal of Research in Marketing.
  2. Price / value perception items — how the price gap read as an anchor and a value signal — adapted from Choi, S. C., & Turut, O. (2023), as above.
  3. Brand-switching intention items — willingness to consider, try, replace, and repurchase the private-label option — adapted from Bansal, H. S., Taylor, S. F., & St. James, Y. (2005). "Migrating" to new service providers: Toward a unifying framework of consumers' switching behaviors. Journal of the Academy of Marketing Science, 33(1), 96–115, adapted here to brand-level (rather than platform-level) switching.
