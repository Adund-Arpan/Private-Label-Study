Inside the Noodle Aisle

A short study on how quick-commerce apps show you products. Takes about 3 minutes.

Academic research only · Responses are anonymous · No real product brands are shown or evaluated

Every attitudinal / measurement item in Section B is adapted from the sources below (marked inline with [1]/[2]/[3]). Screening and demographic questions are standard instrument scaffolding, not measured constructs, so they are not individually cited.

  1. Long, F., & Amaldoss, W. (2024). Self-preferencing in e-commerce marketplaces: The role of sponsored advertising and private labels. Marketing Science, 43(5), 925–952. — source for the forced product-choice outcome and manipulation-check items.
  2. Choi, S. C., & Turut, O. (2023). National brand's competition with premium private labels: The role of context-dependent preferences. International Journal of Research in Marketing. — source for manipulation-check, price-anchoring/value, and switching-intention items.
  3. Bansal, H. S., Taylor, S. F., & St. James, Y. (2005). "Migrating" to new service providers: Toward a unifying framework of consumers' switching behaviors. Journal of the Academy of Marketing Science, 33(1), 96–115. — base scale for the switching-intention items, adapted here to brand-level (rather than platform-level) switching.
